Category guide
Maintenance categories
Oil & filters
Oil changes, oil filter, air filter, cabin filter, fuel filter, and similar filter replacements.
Tires
Tire changes, rotations, balancing, alignment, tire repairs, and seasonal tire swaps.
Brakes
Brake pads, discs or rotors, brake fluid, calipers, brake inspections, and braking-system work.
Inspection
Technical inspections, emissions checks, roadworthiness checks, and pre-trip inspections.
Fluids
Coolant, transmission fluid, brake fluid, washer fluid, power steering fluid, differential oil, and other fluid top-ups or replacements.
Battery
Battery replacement, charging issues, alternator or battery diagnostics, and related electrical checks.
Repair
General mechanical or electrical repairs that do not fit a more specific maintenance category.
Other
Anything that does not clearly belong elsewhere, such as accessories, cleaning, diagnostics, paperwork, or one-off maintenance.
